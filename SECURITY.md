# Security Disclosure Policy

## Scope

This policy applies to security vulnerabilities in any code, tooling, or specifications maintained in this repository.

It does **not** cover vulnerabilities in AI systems or third-party tools *referenced* in our documents — those should be reported directly to their respective maintainers.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

**Please do not report security vulnerabilities through public GitHub issues.**

Email [security@secureaialliance.org](mailto:security@secureaialliance.org) with:

1. **Description** — a clear explanation of the vulnerability
2. **Impact** — what could an attacker do?
3. **Reproduction steps** — how to reproduce the issue
4. **Affected versions** — which releases or commits are affected
5. **Mitigations** — any known workarounds

## Response Timeline

| Milestone | Target |
|---|---|
| Acknowledge receipt | Within 72 hours |
| Confirm vulnerability | Within 7 days |
| Release fix / advisory | Within 90 days, coordinated with reporter |
| Public disclosure | After fix is available, coordinated with reporter |

We will keep you informed throughout the process and credit you in the advisory unless you prefer to remain anonymous.

## Responsible Disclosure

We ask that you:
- Give us reasonable time to address the issue before public disclosure
- Avoid accessing or modifying data belonging to others
- Act in good faith

We commit to:
- Respond promptly and keep you updated
- Not pursue legal action against researchers acting in good faith
- Credit you publicly (if desired)
